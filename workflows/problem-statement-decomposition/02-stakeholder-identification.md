# Step 2: Stakeholder Identification

## Objective
Identify all relevant stakeholders and understand their perspectives, interests, and concerns related to the problem.

## Context
Understanding who is affected by or has interest in the problem is crucial for comprehensive analysis. Different stakeholders may view the same problem differently.

## Instructions

### For AI Agents
1. Analyze the problem statement from Step 1
2. Identify all potential stakeholder groups
3. Categorize stakeholders by their relationship to the problem
4. Document each stakeholder's likely perspective and concerns
5. Prioritize stakeholders based on impact and influence

### For Human Analysts
1. Review the validated problem statement
2. Brainstorm all individuals, groups, or entities that are:
   - Directly affected by the problem
   - Responsible for addressing the problem
   - Have decision-making authority
   - Provide resources or support
   - May be impacted by solutions
3. Document their perspectives and concerns

## Input Requirements
- **Validated Problem Statement** (from Step 1)
- **Organizational Context**: Understanding of the organization structure
- **Domain Knowledge**: Relevant industry or domain information
